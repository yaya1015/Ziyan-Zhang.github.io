Biography

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Anhui University, where I also completed my undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral studies. I received my Ph.D. in computer science in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Bo Jiang and Prof. Bin Luo. My research focuses on robust graph representation learning and graph foundation models. The highest number of Google citations for published papers reached 841 (Google hyperlinks on Digihome). I serve as a reviewer for Pattern Recognition (PR).

张紫砚，现为安徽大学计算机科学与技术学院博士后。本科、硕士和博士均就读于该学院，2024 年获博士学位，博士期间师从江波教授、罗斌教授。主要研究方向为鲁棒图表示学习和图基础模型。已发表论文在 Google Scholar 上的单篇最高引用数达 841 次。担任《Pattern Recognition》（PR）期刊审稿人。

Research Interests

  • Robust Graph Foundation Models. Robust pretraining and efficient fine-tuning for graph tasks.
